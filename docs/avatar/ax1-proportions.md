# AX-1 proportions and signing-space targets

All dimensions use Blender metres. The generated base model is an upper-body study with the neck origin at approximately 1.95 m and the crown at approximately 2.30 m.

| Region | Design target | Rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| Head width | 0.60 m | Visor remains legible without dominating the torso. |
| Neck to shoulder span | 0.95 m | Maintains a human-like frame and arm clearance. |
| Torso width | 0.88 m | Leaves a high-contrast presentation area for hands. |
| Upper arm | 0.38 m | Supports natural elbow reach. |
| Forearm | 0.35 m | Keeps wrists near the chest signing space. |
| Palm | 0.21 × 0.27 m | Intentionally readable for handshape review. |
| Fingers | 0.13–0.20 m | Three segments per finger; independent future joints. |

Neutral pose targets: elbows are slightly flexed; palms are forward/medial enough to remain visible; fingertips do not intersect the chest. No collision rule may forbid normal arm crossing. Final values require review after the rig and camera framing are approved.
